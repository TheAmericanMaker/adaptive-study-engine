# Adaptive Study Schedule

This file is auto-generated by `scripts/generate-schedule.py`.

To populate it, first initialize tracking against your `resources/objectives.yaml`:

```bash
python3 scripts/reset-tracking.py --exam-date YYYY-MM-DD
python3 scripts/generate-schedule.py
```

Then re-run `scripts/generate-schedule.py` after every session — or let
`scripts/log-session.py` invoke it for you.

Do not hand-edit this file; your edits will be overwritten.
